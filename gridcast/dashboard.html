DiagnosticValueInterpretation

KS and Cramér–von Mises test the null of equal distributions. Holm correction covers six tests per vintage. Their p-values assume independent observations; 15-minute demand is autocorrelated, so these are exploratory flags, not calibrated alarms or proof of model deterioration. PSI above 0.20 is a review signal. Seasonal residuals subtract training mean demand for the same quarter-hour of the week. Short windows and serial correlation can inflate apparent change. Backtest observations are later historical actuals; prospective mode requires newly ingested data.
